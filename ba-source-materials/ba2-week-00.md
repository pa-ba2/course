Companies using Business Analytics :
Amazon : uses customer data analysis to recommend products to users based on their past
purchases, searches, and market trends.

Netflix: utilizes viewing data analysis to recommend movies and series to subscribers, as well as
to produce original content based on user preferences.

Uber: uses location and traffic data analysis to optimize routes, determine real-time fares, and
forecast demand in different areas.

Google: uses search data analysis to personalize search results, target online advertisements,
and develop new products and services based on user needs.
